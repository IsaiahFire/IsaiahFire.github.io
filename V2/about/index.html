About me (Personally)

I was born and raised in Buffalo, NY and am currently in high school, deeply involved in every production my school puts on, my media major classes, and making the school a better place. I first got into media production when I got a camera as a kid - photographing flowers moved into making a charity with my sister during the pandemic (as well as a satirical news show), and I've just never stopped creating videos and photos. At school, I help to teach younger media students, produce our news show, and serve as the lead student lighting technician for our many productions.

I can be a super professional human, but I also love to be silly! I've made zines about the art of applying googly eyes to things, love photoshopping crazy things, and have made entire films starring baguettes. In my free time, I love to hang out with my cats, read, and create overcomplicated electronic birthday cards.

Beyond my skills above, I also have a few values I hold close - I believe every situation is a learning experience and should be treated as such, and that ultimately the most fulfilling thing in life is helping the world - my mission as I get ready to study engineering in college is to apply all of the skills and knowledge that I will possess to leave the world a better place.
